# Privacy Policy – EventMark

## Introduction

This privacy policy explains how the **EventMark** mobile application (the "App") handles information about its users. The App is developed and published by **\[Developer or company name\]** ("we", "us").

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

## Summary

The App does not collect, store, process or share any personal data from its users. You do not need to create an account or provide any personal information to use it.

We do not sell data, share it with third parties or use it for advertising, because we simply do not receive any.

## Information we do not collect

The App does not collect or transmit, either to us or to any third party:

- Name, email address, phone number or other contact details
- Precise or approximate location
- Contacts, photos, videos, files or messages stored on the device
- Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
- Usage, browsing or purchase history
- Financial or health information

If the App saves any settings or preferences (for example, language or theme), that information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to our servers or to any third party, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

## Device permissions

The App does not request permissions that give it access to personal information, such as location, contacts, camera or microphone. If a future version needs a permission for a specific feature, it will be used only for that feature on your device, and we will update this policy to explain it.

## Third-party services

The App does not include any third-party services that collect data, such as advertising networks, analytics tools or crash reporting tools.

The App is downloaded and installed through Google Play, which may collect information under its own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). That processing is controlled by Google, not by us.

## Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal data from any user, including children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal data by other means, such as email, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no information about you on our servers that could be exposed. Anything the App stores locally stays under your control, on your device.

## Data retention and your rights

Data protection laws in many countries give you the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Since we do not collect any data, we hold no information about you to which these rights would apply.

If you contact us by email, we will use your address only to reply to you and will delete the message once it is no longer needed.
